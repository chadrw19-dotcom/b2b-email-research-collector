Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who this covers

B2B Email Research Collector is used only with one Gmail account: the study's dedicated research inbox. No other Google account is authorized to use it.

What Google data it accesses

Read-only access to that inbox's email messages (Gmail scope gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or modify email, and it accesses no other Google data.

What it does with that data

  • It copies messages from that inbox into the study's private research database. Those messages are company newsletters the inbox subscribed to, plus their confirmation emails.
  • It analyzes them for aggregate research findings, such as sending frequency, content types and calls to action.
  • Published findings are aggregate. Full emails are not published, and personal data that appears in an email (for example a sender's signature) is not published.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. It is stored in a private repository that only the study team can access.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Raw messages are deleted 12 months after the study's findings are published. Only aggregate data is kept after that. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
