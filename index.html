B2B Email Research Collector

This is the collection tool for a research study of B2B email marketing. The study subscribes a dedicated research inbox to free company newsletters. It then analyzes the marketing emails those companies send: how often, what kind of content, and which calls to action.

The tool has one user: the study's own Gmail inbox. It is not offered to the public, and nobody can sign up for it.

It uses read-only Gmail access to copy the emails that arrive in that inbox into the study's private database. It never sends, deletes or changes email.
